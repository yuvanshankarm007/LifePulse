## 💡 Quick Preview Recording Checklist
1. **Screen Resolution:** 1920x1080 (1080p) 60 FPS using Windows Game Bar (`Win + G`) or OBS.
2. **Controls:**
   - **Click top 25% of preview:** Toggles between all 6 emergency modes.
   - **Click center/target:** Simulates compressions, thrusts, or pressure.
   - **WASD + Q/E:** Smooth camera walking and kneeling.
3. Keep the video under 2 minutes, upload to YouTube as Public or Unlisted, and submit!
