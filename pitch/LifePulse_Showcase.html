🚨 4 Multi-Emergency Protocols

❤️ Cardiac Arrest

110 BPM metronome pulse, 30:2 counter, rescue breaths, and AED holographic pad targets.

🗣️ Severe Choking

5 Back blows followed by 5 inward-upward Heimlich abdominal thrusts at navel level.

🩸 Severe Bleeding

Direct wound pressure hold gauge and 2-3 inch tourniquet placement line.

💊 Opioid Overdose

Narcan nasal spray guidance and recovery position lateral recumbent roll guide.
