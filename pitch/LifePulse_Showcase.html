🚨 6 Comprehensive Bystander Emergency Protocols

❤️ 1. Cardiac Arrest (Adult/Child/Infant)

110 BPM metronome pulse, 30:2 counter, rescue breaths, AED targets, and 2-min rescuer fatigue swap alert.

🗣️ 2. Severe Choking (Heimlich)

5 Back blows + 5 abdominal thrusts at navel level. Automatic fallback to unconscious CPR if victim collapses.

🩸 3. Severe Arterial Bleeding

Direct wound pressure hold gauge and 2-3" tourniquet line with second tourniquet escalation if bleeding continues.

💊 4. Opioid Overdose (Narcan)

Narcan nasal spray guidance + recovery position lateral recumbent roll guide + CPR fallback.

⚡ 5. Anaphylaxis (EpiPen)

Outer mid-thigh auto-injector guide with 3-second firm hold and 10-second site massage.

🧠 6. Active Seizure & Convulsions

Perimeter hazard clearing boundary + duration timer with status epilepticus alert (>5 min).

🎯 Dual Intelligence Architecture

  • AI Auto-Detect Scanner: Assesses scene posture, chest rise, and trauma signs in 1.5 seconds on launch.
  • Natural Voice Recognition: Bystanders can shout "Start CPR!", "He's choking!", or "Severe bleeding!" for instant hands-free switching.
  • Demographic Tuning: Voice or tap toggling for Adult (2 hands), Child (1 hand), and Infant (2 fingers).
