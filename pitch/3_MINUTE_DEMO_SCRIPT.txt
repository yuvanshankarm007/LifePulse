================================================================================
LIFEPULSE - 3-MINUTE HACKATHON DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT
Snap Spectacles & Devpost "Learn AR with SPECS" Hackathon
Project by: Yuvan Shankar
================================================================================

RECORDING SETUP CHECKLIST:
- Resolution: 1080p or 1440p (Full Screen)
- Recording Tool: Windows Game Bar (Win + G) or OBS Studio
- Windows Open:
  1. Lens Studio (with Preview 1 active showing the 3D patient and AR HUD)
  2. Browser tab at http://localhost:3000/ (LifePulse Live AI Workbench)
- Target Duration: 2 minutes 50 seconds to 3 minutes 05 seconds

================================================================================
PART 1: THE HOOK & THE PROBLEM (0:00 - 0:30)
================================================================================

[SCREEN / ACTION]:
Start full-screen on Lens Studio Preview 1. The scene shows the 3D patient 
mannequin on the floor, surrounded by the holographic AR HUD and glowing cyan ring.

[VOICEOVER / WHAT TO SAY]:
"Every year, over 350,000 people suffer sudden cardiac arrest outside of a 
hospital. Survival drops by 10% for every single minute CPR is delayed. 

When someone collapses, chokes, or bleeds heavily in front of you, our instinct 
is to reach for a smartphone. But in a life-or-death crisis, smartphones 
fundamentally fail. 

You need two hands to pump a chest with 50 kilograms of force. You need two 
hands to perform the Heimlich maneuver or wrap a tourniquet. You cannot hold or 
swipe a phone while giving life-saving physical care. And you can't take your 
eyes off the patient.

This is LifePulse—a hands-free, spatial emergency first responder companion built 
natively for Snap Spectacles."


================================================================================
PART 2: CARDIAC ARREST & 110 BPM CPR GUIDANCE (0:30 - 1:15)
================================================================================

[SCREEN / ACTION]:
1. Move the camera slightly forward using 'W' or orbit with right-click drag 
   to look at the patient's chest.
2. Click the center glowing reticle multiple times to pump compressions. 
   Notice the 110 BPM metronome pulsing, the compression counter incrementing 
   (1, 2, 3... 30), and the elastic depth recoil bounce.

[VOICEOVER / WHAT TO SAY]:
"LifePulse utilizes all three core spatial modalities: it perceives where the 
victim is lying, projects 3D holographic guidance directly in the physical world, 
and leaves your hands completely unencumbered.

Right now, we are in Cardiac Arrest mode. Centered over the sternum is an 18-centimeter 
holographic target pulsing at exactly 110 beats per minute—the clinical sweet spot 
recommended by the American Heart Association.

As I perform chest compressions, the system tracks my cadence and counts up to 
30 before signaling two rescue breaths. Notice how the reticle bounces with simulated 
depth recoil. 

We also solved a major clinical edge case: rescuer fatigue. Compressing a chest 
exhausts anyone in under 2 minutes, causing compressions to become dangerously 
shallow. LifePulse automatically tracks elapsed CPR time and alerts you to swap 
rescuers every 120 seconds, maintaining vital perfusion pressure."


================================================================================
PART 3: MULTI-EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS & VOICE COMMANDS (1:15 - 2:00)
================================================================================

[SCREEN / ACTION]:
1. Click the top header of the HUD to cycle through the emergencies:
   - Click once -> CHOKING (Target moves to navel / upper abdomen)
   - Click again -> SEVERE BLEEDING (Target moves to limb pressure point)
   - Click again -> OVERDOSE (Guides Narcan spray and recovery position)
   - Click again -> ANAPHYLAXIS (Outer thigh EpiPen guide)
   - Click again -> SEIZURE (Perimeter clearance zone and timer)

[VOICEOVER / WHAT TO SAY]:
"Real emergencies aren't just cardiac arrests. LifePulse supports a full spectrum 
of bystander crises, controllable completely hands-free via voice commands or quick 
pinch gestures.

- For Severe Choking, the target shifts up to the navel, guiding 5 firm back blows 
  followed by 5 upward Heimlich thrusts. If the victim loses consciousness, it 
  instantly flips guidance back to ground CPR.
- For Arterial Bleeding, it locks onto the limb, displaying a real-time pressure 
  hold gauge and showing exactly where to place a tourniquet 2 to 3 inches above the wound.
- For Opioid Overdoses, it guides single-dose Narcan nasal administration and shows 
  how to roll the victim into the lateral recovery position so their airway stays clear.
- It even covers severe allergic anaphylaxis with mid-thigh EpiPen timing, and active 
  seizures with a glowing safety perimeter to prevent head trauma."


================================================================================
PART 4: LIVE GENERATIVE AI TRIAGE & ZERO-CRASH RESILIENCE (2:00 - 2:40)
================================================================================

[SCREEN / ACTION]:
Alt-Tab smoothly over to your browser showing the Live AI Workbench at 
http://localhost:3000/.
1. Click one of the quick test chips (e.g., 'Arterial Bleed' or 'Overdose').
2. Point out the sub-1.5 second live response from the Groq / Gemini AI model.
3. Show the generated JSON parameters: exact 3D coordinates, warnings, and 
   contraindications ("DO NOTs").

[VOICEOVER / WHAT TO SAY]:
"To handle unusual and complex accidents where bystanders have no idea what to do, 
LifePulse connects to a live Generative AI Clinical Triage gateway running on Groq 
and Google Gemini.

Watch this: if a bystander speaks a natural language description like 'He fell off 
a ladder and has a deep cut spurting blood', the AI model interprets the situation 
in under 1.5 seconds. 

It generates the exact 3D anatomical anchor transforms for the Spectacles waveguide, 
specifies the kinetic action step, and extracts vital clinical contraindications—such 
as 'DO NOT remove the tourniquet' or 'DO NOT force objects into a seizing mouth'.

And for live demo resilience, LifePulse features a Zero-Crash Guarantee. If the 
internet drops or the glasses go offline, an embedded local clinical engine handles 
all 15 major protocols in under 1 millisecond with zero latency."
